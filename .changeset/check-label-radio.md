Checkbox, radio and label follow the Figma Check and Label components. `GCheckbox` and the new `GRadioGroup` / `GRadioGroupItem` are drawn as Lucide outline glyphs (square / circle) that turn purple with a check when on, preview the next state on hover and show a minus when indeterminate. `GLabel` is 14px semibold with a `#c10007` required asterisk, an `info` slot, and a quiet gray disabled state instead of transparency. Adds the `check-glyph-*` mask utilities.
