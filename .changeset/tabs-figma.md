**Breaking:** `GTabsList` follows the Figma **Tabs** page. `variant` is now `'toggle-primary'` (default, was `'base'`), `'toggle-secondary'` (new) or `'tab'` (was `'outline'`). Hover and press use Figma's alpha overlays, the `tab` variant draws a 1px `bni/border` line, 16px spacing, a 3px `primary` underline and `primary/primary-quiet` on hover, and the toggle items use 16px semibold labels.
