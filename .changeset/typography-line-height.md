The `text-display-*`, `text-heading-*`, `text-body-*` and `text-action-*` utilities set the line height of their Figma style: `normal` (Figma's "Auto") for all of them, and 1.5 for `text-body-md` and `text-body-mdEm`. Before, they inherited Tailwind's line height for each size.
